# Hi, I'm Danish Puri

I study computer engineering at NYU and I like building computer vision systems that have to work outside the lab, on real cameras, weak networks, and messy traffic.

My main project is [GateWatch](https://github.com/danish-puri/GateWatch), which logs the vehicles and people crossing a college gate in Kathmandu, Nepal, from its CCTV cameras. It's the second version. I ran the first one, [bird](https://github.com/danish-puri/bird), at two campuses and wrote it up in the [LibertyTrack case study](https://osf.io/6s7aw/files/9kch3).

I also built [PrepMate](https://github.com/danish-puri/prepmate), which scouts chess opponents from their public games, and [imaGine](https://github.com/danish-puri/imaGine), which lets you draw in the air with your finger through a webcam.
